Research directions

Trustworthy language models

Personalize LLMs from a few preference examples and use agreement across models to evaluate agent-generated annotations without reference labels.

Learning with limited supervision

Use self-teaching, in-context learning, and knowledge distillation when labels are scarce, noisy, or incomplete.

Model attribution and multimodal learning

Trace predictions back to influential training examples and study how models understand images, video, and motion.

Agentic AI and governance

Study how to evaluate the fairness and accountability of AI agents in real settings, including digital labor platforms.
